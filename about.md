I am Sakul Learning, a young and curious learner. This blog is my notebook for careful synthesis: collect sources first, keep drafts unpublished on the generated site, then publish only when a topic has enough evidence and refinement.

## Editorial principles

- Preserve source links and notes before writing conclusions.
- Keep unfinished articles in `_drafts/`, not `_posts/`, so GitHub Pages does not publish them as posts.
- Publish only after the source folder for a topic has sufficient material.
- Favor concise summaries, clear caveats, and links back to primary sources.
