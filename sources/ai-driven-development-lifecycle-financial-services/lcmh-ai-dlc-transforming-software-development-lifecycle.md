# AI-DLC: how AI is transforming the software development lifecycle

Source: <https://lcmh.fr/en/articles/2026/ai-dlc-transforming-software-development-lifecycle/>

## Why this source matters

This source adds a broader angle to the AWS financial-services AI-DLC draft. Instead of focusing mainly on regulated financial-services delivery, it presents AI-DLC as a general software-development lifecycle shift: AI can initiate workflows, break work into plans, and execute tasks while humans validate the result.

## Key points to reuse

- AI-DLC is not just code assistance; it applies across design, implementation, testing, security, operations, and production support.
- The collaboration loop is: AI proposes a plan, stakeholders validate it, AI executes, and humans verify the result.
- Developers shift from being mainly executors to decision-makers, reviewers, validators, and risk owners.
- Adaptive workflows matter because small bug fixes and major architecture changes should not receive the same process depth.
- The article cautions that traditional productivity measures such as lines of code and velocity do not necessarily capture business value.
- Reported case studies are useful evidence, but should be paired with questions about prerequisites such as modular code, clear specifications, review discipline, and human accountability.

## How it changes the draft angle

The draft should not only say that AI-DLC helps regulated teams move faster with governance. It can also ask a broader question: if AI becomes the planner and executor across more of the lifecycle, what new work remains for humans? The likely answer is judgment, validation, architecture, risk review, and deciding when automation should stop.

## Working questions

- Which parts of the AWS financial-services model apply to ordinary software teams?
- What evidence separates real productivity improvement from more generated output?
- How much human validation is enough when AI initiates work rather than merely responding to prompts?
- What team practices make AI-generated lifecycle work reviewable and safe?
